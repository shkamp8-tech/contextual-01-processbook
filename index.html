📘 Welcome to my Process Book

An interactive canvas documenting my creative process — research, ideas, conversations, and connections.

🧭 How to navigate

  • Drag the empty canvas to pan around.
  • Scroll the mouse wheel to zoom toward the cursor.
  • On touch: drag with one finger to pan, pinch with two fingers to zoom.
  • Click the + / − / ⌂ buttons in the top bar to zoom in, out, or reset.

🔑 Pin codes

Some cards link to other parts of my work and have a small pin code in the bottom right. Use the same access code system if you encounter another locked screen — the pin codes you find here unlock those.

⚠️ Disclaimer

This process book is still under construction. Dates, descriptions, links and the overall layout may not yet be final. Some sections are placeholders. You're seeing a snapshot of work in progress.
